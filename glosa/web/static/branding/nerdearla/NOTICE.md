# Nerdearla logos

The files in this folder (`nerdearla-logo.svg`, `nerdearla-logo-bw.svg`) are trademarks of Nerdearla.

- The organizers shared them with the participants of the Nerdearla Vibeathon 2026 on the official brand assets page (https://nerdear.live/logos).
- They are included here only so that the demo shows Glosa branded for Nerdearla.
- They are **not** covered by this repository's Apache-2.0 license.
- To use Glosa at another event, replace them with your own logo through `branding.logo_url` in `config.yaml`.

## Brand usage notes (from Nerdearla's brand manual, May 2021)

- **Official palette:**
  - yellow `#FFBA00`
  - teal `#00ACA8`
  - red `#FF323C`

  The SVGs use close variants: `#fcb316`, `#03a6a2`, `#ef3b42` and black `#010101`.
- **Never distort the logo:** do not stretch it, condense it, tilt it, or change its proportions, inner spacing, palette or lettering.
- **Pick the version by background:**
  - on a light background (lighter than 50 % grey), use the positive version;
  - on a dark background, use the negative version.

  Both SVGs here are "sticker" versions with a thick black outline, so they read well on Glosa's dark theme.
- **Typefaces:** Rift Soft (primary) and Roboto (secondary). Glosa keeps its own type and uses only the logo.
